Engine Oil Change

5 StepsSame order as the simulation

Step 1 · Prepare Tools & Safety Check
Verify the engine is completely OFF
Ensure the engine has cooled to avoid burns
Confirm parking brake is engaged on a level surface
Wear gloves, goggles, and protective gear
Prepare the correct oil type and tools
Engine must be cool and completely OFF — hot oil and parts cause serious burns.
Step 2 · Drain the Old Oil
Locate the oil drain plug under the chassis
Position the drain pan directly under the plug
Loosen and remove the drain plug slowly
Allow the used oil to drain completely
Avoid direct contact with used oil — wear gloves and keep the drain pan stable.
Step 3 · Reinstall Plug & Replace Filter
Reinstall the drain plug and tighten to the correct torque
Remove the old oil filter carefully
Lubricate the new filter gasket with fresh oil
Install the new oil filter hand-tight only
Check that the old gasket is fully removed
Hand-tighten the filter only — overtightening damages the gasket and causes leaks.
Step 4 · Refill Engine Oil
Remove the oil filler cap
Pour the recommended oil type slowly to prevent spills
Wipe the dipstick before checking the level
Check that the oil level is within the safe range
Use only the recommended oil type — wrong viscosity can damage the engine.
Step 5 · Test Engine & Clean Up
Start the engine and let it run briefly
Inspect the drain plug and filter area for leaks
Stop the engine immediately if leaks appear
Never dump used oil — store it sealed and bring it to a recycling center.
