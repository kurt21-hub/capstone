Brake Pad Replacement
Matches the practice simulation — tick each action as you perform it
5 StepsSame order as the simulation
Student Vehicle Date Axle
Step 1 · Prepare Tools & Safety Check
Wear gloves, goggles, and protective gear
Prepare jack, wrench, and correct socket sizes
Remove the front tire
Wear PPE and keep the vehicle stable on a level surface before lifting.
Step 2 · Lift Vehicle & Remove Wheel
Inspect the lifting point under the vehicle
Lift and secure the vehicle with the jack stand
Never work under a vehicle supported only by a jack — always use jack stands.
Step 3 · Inspect & Remove Old Pads
Inspect the brake disc, pads, and caliper for wear or damage
Remove the caliper with the socket wrench
Remove the worn brake pads
Support the caliper — never let it hang by the brake hose; hose damage causes brake failure.
Step 4 · Install New Pads & Reassemble
Install the new brake pads
Reinstall the caliper and tighten the bolts
Reinstall the wheel and hand-tighten the nuts
Torque the wheel nuts in a star pattern
Torque wheel nuts in a star pattern — uneven torque warps the rotor and wheel.
Step 5 · Pump Pedal & Test Brakes
Pump the brake pedal until it feels firm
Check the brake fluid level
Start the engine and listen for unusual noises
Always pump the pedal before driving — the first press after pad replacement can sink to the floor.
