Fuel Filter Replacement
Matches the practice simulation — tick each action as you perform it
5 StepsSame order as the simulation
Student Vehicle Date Filter loc.
Step 1 · Safety & Locate the Filter
Confirm ignition is OFF and keys are removed
Disconnect the battery negative terminal first
Locate the fuel filter mounted on the firewall
Wear gloves and safety goggles
Prepare wrenches, rag, and the new filter
Disconnect the battery first — a single spark near fuel vapor can ignite a fire.
Step 2 · Relieve Pressure & Disconnect Lines
Loosen the top banjo bolt slowly to relieve fuel pressure
Use a rag to catch any fuel spray
Remove the top banjo bolt completely
Remove the lower fuel line fitting with a wrench
Relieve pressure slowly — pressurized fuel sprays and is highly flammable.
Step 3 · Replace the Fuel Filter
Unscrew the mounting bracket bolt
Slide the old fuel filter out of the bracket
Place the new filter in the bracket
Never lose the washers — missing washers cause fuel leaks.
Step 4 · Reconnect Lines & Tighten
Reconnect the bottom fuel line first
Then reinstall the top banjo bolt
Tighten fittings to proper torque — firm, not over-tightened
Reconnect the battery negative terminal
Bottom line first, then the banjo bolt — wrong order complicates reassembly.
Step 5 · Prime & Test the Engine
Turn the key to ON to build fuel pressure — do not start yet
Start the engine and let it run
Confirm the engine runs smoothly
Retighten any leaking fittings if needed
Check for leaks before driving — a fuel leak is a serious fire hazard.
