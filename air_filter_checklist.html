Air Filter Replacement
Matches the practice simulation — tick each action as you perform it
5 StepsSame order as the simulation
Student Vehicle Date Odometer
Step 1 · Prepare & Open the Hood
Confirm the engine is OFF and completely cool
Pull the hood latch and secure the hood open
Locate the black plastic air filter box near the engine
Identify the large air intake hose connected to the box
Prepare the new filter and basic tools
Engine must be OFF and cool — a hot engine causes burns.
Step 2 · Open Housing & Remove Filter
Release the metal clips or remove the housing screws
Pull the housing cover off and set it aside
Take out the old air filter gently
Handle the cover gently — plastic clips can snap if forced.
Step 3 · Inspect & Clean Housing
Check inside the housing for dust and dirt
Wipe the housing with a clean cloth if needed
Make sure the housing is completely clean
Never let debris fall into the intake — it goes straight into the engine.
Step 4 · Install the New Filter
Position the new filter the same way as the old one
Make sure it fits snugly with no gaps around the edges
A poorly seated filter lets unfiltered air into the engine — check for gaps.
Step 5 · Secure Housing & Final Check
Put the housing cover back in place
Lock the metal clips or tighten the screws
Close the hood securely
Start the engine and check that it runs smoothly
Make sure the cover is fully sealed — an open housing lets dirt in over time.
