Engine Oil Change
Complete service procedure — preparation to disposal
18 Lessons ~75 min total · Easy–Medium
1Overview

This guide covers the complete engine oil change process from start to finish — the same procedure taught in the Engine Oil Change learning module, practice simulation, and assessment simulation.

What you will learn:

  • Why regular oil changes are essential for engine health.
  • The tools and materials required for a safe oil change.
  • The 14-step procedure from preparation to disposal.
2Tools & Materials

Gather all necessary tools and materials before starting.

Oil drain pan (holds 4–5 liters)
Wrench set — 14mm / 17mm
New oil filter
Engine oil — correct grade per manual
Funnel
Gloves and clean rags
Safety Reminder: Always wear gloves when handling engine oil to protect your skin.
3Procedure — 14 Steps
1Prepare the Vehicle 5 minEasy
Park on a flat surface, engage parking brake, turn off engine and allow to cool.
  1. Park on a flat, level surface and engage parking brake.
  2. Turn off engine and allow to cool 10–15 minutes.
  3. Open hood and secure with support rod.
Never work on a hot engine — allow sufficient cooling time.
2Locate the Drain Plug 5 minEasy
Find the oil drain plug at the bottom of the engine oil pan.
  1. Access the underside of the vehicle using jack stands.
  2. Find the oil pan — flat metal pan at the engine bottom.
  3. Identify the drain plug bolt at the lowest point.
Never work under a vehicle supported only by a floor jack.
3Position the Oil Pan 3 minEasy
Place the drain pan correctly to catch all old oil.
  1. Place drain pan directly beneath the drain plug.
  2. Ensure pan can hold 4–5 liters.
  3. Position slightly forward — oil may shoot at an angle.
4Remove the Drain Plug 5 minEasy
Use the correct wrench to remove the drain plug and begin draining.
  1. Select correct wrench size (14mm or 17mm).
  2. Turn counterclockwise to loosen.
  3. Remove fully by hand — keep clear of oil flow.
  4. Set plug aside in a safe, clean location.
Warm oil can splash — wear gloves and keep hands clear.
5Drain the Old Oil 10 minEasy
Allow old oil to fully drain into the pan before filter removal.
  1. Let oil drain completely — 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. Wait until only a slow drip remains.
  3. Inspect drain plug washer — replace if worn.
6Remove the Old Oil Filter 7 minMedium
Remove the old filter while oil is still draining.
  1. Locate oil filter on side of engine block.
  2. Use oil filter wrench to loosen counterclockwise.
  3. Remove by hand — tilt to avoid spilling.
Old filter contains residual oil — handle carefully.
7Prepare the New Filter 3 minEasy
Apply new oil to the gasket of the new filter to ensure a proper seal.
  1. Unpack the new oil filter.
  2. Apply a thin film of new oil to the rubber gasket ring.
  3. This ensures proper seal and prevents leaks.
8Install the New Filter 5 minMedium
Thread and hand-tighten the new oil filter into place.
  1. Thread new filter clockwise by hand.
  2. Tighten ¾ turn after gasket contacts engine.
  3. Hand tight only — do not use a wrench.
Overtightening damages the gasket and causes leaks.
9Reinstall the Drain Plug 5 minEasy
Clean and reinstall the drain plug securely before adding new oil.
  1. Wipe drain plug and area clean.
  2. Install new washer if old one is worn.
  3. Thread plug clockwise by hand, then snug with wrench.
A loose drain plug causes immediate oil leak on start.
10Locate the Filler Cap 2 minEasy
Find the oil filler cap on top of the engine.
  1. Look on top of engine for cap with oil can symbol.
  2. Twist counterclockwise to remove.
  3. Place cap in a clean area.
11Pour in New Oil 5 minEasy
Refill the engine with the correct amount and type of new oil.
  1. Place funnel into filler opening.
  2. Pour specified amount of oil slowly.
  3. Check vehicle manual for correct quantity.
Do not overfill — causes pressure build-up and seal damage.
12Check Oil Level 3 minEasy
Use the dipstick to verify oil level is correct.
  1. Pull dipstick out and wipe clean.
  2. Reinsert fully, then pull out again.
  3. Level should be between MIN and MAX marks.
  4. Add more in small amounts if needed.
13Replace the Filler Cap 2 minEasy
Securely replace the oil filler cap to prevent leaks.
  1. Place filler cap back onto the opening.
  2. Twist clockwise until secure.
  3. Wipe any spilled oil off the engine.
14Start Engine & Verify 5 minEasy
Start the engine, check for leaks, and verify oil pressure.
  1. Start engine and run for 1–2 minutes.
  2. Check oil pressure light — should turn off in seconds.
  3. Inspect drain plug and filter for leaks.
  4. Turn off, wait 5 min, recheck dipstick level.
  5. Dispose old oil at approved recycling center.
Never pour old oil down the drain — it is hazardous waste.

5Key Takeaways
Always ensure the engine is cool before starting.
Replace the oil filter every time.
Use the correct oil grade from the vehicle manual.
Check oil level with the dipstick — between MIN and MAX.
Dispose of old oil responsibly at a recycling center.
